Our Story

From a single espresso machine to a neighborhood favorite.

Since 2019

Born from a love of slow mornings.

MuraTara Café started as a weekend idea between three friends who believed coffee should be made with patience, not speed. We opened our doors in 2019 with one espresso machine, a vintage grinder, and a handwritten menu pinned to the wall.

Today, we still roast in small batches, still know our regulars by name, and still pour every cup like it matters — because it does.

  • Ethically sourced, single-origin beans
  • Pastries baked fresh every morning
  • A no-rush, laptop-friendly space
  • Plant-based milk options at no extra cost
  • Local artists featured on our walls
Our promise

"Good coffee, made with care, shared with kindness."
